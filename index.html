Matcha Latte

Ceremonial-grade matcha whisked to a smooth paste, poured over steamed oat milk and finished with a dusting of matcha powder. Earthy, naturally sweet, and dialled for balance — not too grassy, not too milky. A DON MACCHIATOS staple since day one.

Milk Oat / dairy

Item No. 002 — Espresso-based — House favourite available daily

Vanilla Bean Latte

Double shot espresso with house-made vanilla bean syrup and steamed whole milk. We scrape real vanilla pods — no extract, no shortcuts. The sweetness is restrained; the bean specks in the foam are the tell. Warm, smooth, and consistently the most-reordered drink on the menu.

Base Double espresso
Syrup House vanilla bean
Temp Hot / Iced
Status House favourite

Sec. 03 — About

DON MACCHIATOS began in October 2022 as a single kiosk in Sambag 2, Cebu City — founded by Ariel Alegado and Nickie San Juan with one clear goal: bring specialty-quality coffee to students and everyday Filipinos at a price that actually makes sense.

The brand pioneered Cebu's ₱39 coffee movement, proving that premium taste and accessibility are not opposites. Word spread fast. By September 2023, Don Macchiatos had grown to over 600 branches nationwide — spanning Cebu, Palawan, Bohol, and Manila.

The franchise model is built for first-time entrepreneurs. From kiosk to full café format, every franchisee receives complete training, equipment sourcing, supply chain access, and ongoing operational support — everything needed to open and run from day one.
