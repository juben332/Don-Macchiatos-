Item No. 001 — Espresso-based — Signature available daily

Matcha Latte

Ceremonial-grade matcha whisked to a smooth paste, poured over steamed oat milk and finished with a dusting of matcha powder. Earthy, naturally sweet, and dialled for balance — not too grassy, not too milky. A Kanoffee staple since day one.

Base Ceremonial matcha
Milk Oat / dairy
Temp Hot / Iced
Status Signature
Item No. 002 — Espresso-based — House favourite available daily

Vanilla Bean Latte

Double shot espresso with house-made vanilla bean syrup and steamed whole milk. We scrape real vanilla pods — no extract, no shortcuts. The sweetness is restrained; the bean specks in the foam are the tell. Warm, smooth, and consistently the most-reordered drink on the menu.

Base Double espresso
Syrup House vanilla bean
Temp Hot / Iced
Status House favourite
Item No. 003 — Espresso-based — Bold available daily

Dark Mocha

Triple-shot espresso with 70% dark chocolate sauce, steamed milk, and a thin layer of mocha crema on top. No whipped cream — it would mask the bitterness we worked to calibrate. Rich, intense, and built for people who mean it. Best ordered hot.

Base Triple espresso
Chocolate 70% dark sauce
Temp Hot / Iced
Status Bold
Item No. 004 — Espresso-based — Seasonal available daily

Ube (Purple Yam) Latte

House-made ube halaya blended with steamed milk and a single shot of espresso. The purple yam is sourced locally — cooked down in-house, never from powder. Subtly sweet with a natural creaminess that doesn't need sugar added. The colour is real.

Base Ube halaya
Origin Local purple yam
Temp Hot / Iced
Status Seasonal

Sec. 03 — About

Kanoffee started with one question: why does specialty coffee have to feel pretentious? We wanted a place where the coffee is taken seriously but the experience isn't — somewhere you sit for two hours, order twice, and leave knowing exactly what you drank.

Every bean on the menu is single-origin. Every espresso is dialled fresh at open. The food was built around the coffee — not the other way around. Hollandaise from scratch, batter rested, tiramisu set overnight. Short menu, every item worth ordering.

Opened in 2022. Iterated since. The current space — white marble, warm timber, clean lines — is version three of the fit-out. First two were close but not right. This one is.
